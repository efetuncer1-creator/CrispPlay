CrispPlay subtitle translation model
====================================

Model:    OPUS-MT opus-mt-tc-big-en-tr (English -> Turkish)
Authors:  Helsinki-NLP / Language Technology Research Group, University of Helsinki
Source:   https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-tr (revision e539fc16a8)
License:  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (see LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt)

Papers:
  Tiedemann, J. and Thottingal, S. (2020). OPUS-MT - Building open translation
  services for the World. Proceedings of EAMT 2020.
  Tiedemann, J. et al. (2023). Democratizing neural machine translation with
  OPUS-MT. Language Resources and Evaluation.

Changes made by CrispPlay: the model was converted to the CTranslate2 format with
int8 quantization (ct2-transformers-converter --quantization int8). The weights
were not otherwise modified. source.spm and target.spm are the original
SentencePiece models of the same release.
